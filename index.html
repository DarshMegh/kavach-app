How It Works

Simple. Fast. Silent.

1

Add Emergency Contacts

Add your trusted people — Mom, Dad, best friend. They'll receive SMS + calls during an emergency.

2

Enable Background Detection

Turn on Shake, Scream, and Fall detection. Kavach runs 24/7 in background — even when phone is locked.

3

Emergency Happens

Shake, scream, fall, or press SOS. Kavach starts a 10-second countdown you can cancel if it's accidental.

4

Kavach Responds Instantly

Silent SMS with GPS → Auto-call contacts → Video recording starts. All automatic. All silent.

🔒

100% Private. Zero Servers.

Kavach never sends your data to any server. Everything runs on your phone. SMS goes directly to your contacts. Videos save to your own Google Photos. Your data is yours — always.
